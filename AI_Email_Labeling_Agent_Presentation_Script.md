## Part 2: The Solution & Workflow

So how does it work? Let me walk you through the workflow.

### Step 1 — The Trigger

It all starts the moment a **new email arrives in Gmail**. That's our trigger. No manual intervention, no scheduled batch — it's instant and fully automated.

### Step 2 — The Brain: AI Processing

Instead of a human manually opening, reading, and interpreting every single email, the message is immediately sent to our **Labeling AI Agent**, powered by **Google Gemini**.

This isn't a simple keyword filter. The Gemini model understands **context**, **tone**, and **intent**. It reads the email the way a skilled executive assistant would — comprehending what the sender actually wants.

### Step 3 — The Action: Automatic Categorization

Within **seconds**, the AI classifies the email into one of four business-critical labels:

| Label                  | What It Captures                                                        |
| ---------------------- | ----------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **Sponsorship**        | Sponsorship offers, partnership funding proposals, brand deals           |
| **Collaboration**      | Joint venture requests, co-creation invitations, cross-promotion offers  |
| **Business Inquiries** | Client questions, service requests, pricing inquiries, sales leads       |
| **Others**             | Newsletters, notifications, general correspondence, low-priority items   |

The email is **labeled automatically** inside Gmail. No drag-and-drop, no manual folders — it just happens.

The result? You open your inbox and immediately see a **structured, prioritized view** of what needs your attention.
