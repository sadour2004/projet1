## Part 2: The Solution & Workflow

Let me walk you through how this works. The entire process is **fully automated** and runs in three simple steps.

### Step 1 — The Trigger

It all starts the moment a **new email lands in your Gmail inbox**. That's the trigger. No manual action required — the system is always listening, always ready.

### Step 2 — The Brain (AI Processing)

Instead of a human manually opening, reading, and interpreting every single email, the message is instantly forwarded to our **Labeling AI Agent**.

This agent is powered by **Google Gemini** — one of the most advanced large language models available today. It doesn't just scan for keywords. It **understands context, tone, and intent**. It reads the email the way a skilled executive assistant would — but in a fraction of a second.

### Step 3 — The Action (Automatic Categorization)

Based on its analysis, the AI sorts every incoming email into **one of four business-critical labels:**

| Label                  | What It Captures                                                        |
| ---------------------- | ----------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **Sponsorship**        | Sponsorship offers, partnership proposals, brand deal inquiries          |
| **Collaboration**      | Collaboration requests, joint venture pitches, co-creation invitations  |
| **Business Inquiries** | Client questions, service requests, pricing inquiries, RFPs             |
| **Others**             | Newsletters, notifications, general correspondence, non-priority items  |

The labeling happens **automatically and in seconds**. By the time you open your inbox, every email is already **sorted, categorized, and ready for action**.

No drag-and-drop. No manual folders. No guesswork.
